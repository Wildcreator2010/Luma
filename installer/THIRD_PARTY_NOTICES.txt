Luma full edition — third-party runtime notices
================================================

The "full" edition bundles runtime files copied from a locally installed
Bambu Studio and from the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime so that the printer
camera can work without a separate Bambu Studio installation.

These files are not part of Luma and are not covered by Luma's GPL-3.0-only
license. They remain subject to their own licenses:

Bambu Studio / Bambu network plugin
-----------------------------------
- BambuSource.dll
- live555.dll
- libaosl.dll
- bambu_networking.dll
- agora_rtc_sdk.dll
- libagora-ffmpeg.dll
- libagora-soundtouch.dll

Bambu Studio is licensed under AGPL-3.0. The Bambu networking plugin is
distributed by Bambu Lab and may be non-free; see the Bambu Studio README:
https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio

FFmpeg
------
- avcodec-*.dll
- avutil-*.dll
- swscale-*.dll
- swresample-*.dll

These DLLs come from the Bambu Studio installation. FFmpeg licensing depends
on the build configuration; see https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html.
